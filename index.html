Controls

  • Drag furniture to move it (snaps to 50 mm, hold Shift for 10 mm)
  • Drag a door/window to slide it along its wall
  • Select a wall, then drag it to move it; connected walls stretch with it
  • Orbit: left-drag on empty space · Pan: right-drag · Zoom: wheel
  • R rotate 90° (Shift+R 15°) · D duplicate · Del delete
  • V toggle plan/3D · C cut-away walls · Ctrl+Z undo
  • All sizes are in millimetres. Changes save in this browser automatically.
